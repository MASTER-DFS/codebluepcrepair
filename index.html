Questions

Computer repair questions, answered

Do you take walk-ins?

No. Code Blue works by appointment only, so each repair gets full attention and you aren't left waiting in line.

How much does it cost?

Every job is priced on its own, and home repairs and business support are quoted separately. Code Blue keeps pricing affordable. You'll get an estimate before any repair work starts, and nothing is done without your OK. Call or text for a ballpark before you book.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes. Seniors and veterans get 25% off home jobs. Veteran-owned businesses get 25% off too, with proof of veteran ownership. Just mention it when you book and I'll confirm how it applies.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes. Through Code Blue IT Services I provide outsourced IT for small businesses: networks, workstations, Microsoft 365, backups and security. Business work is quoted separately from home repairs.

How do you handle my files?

I treat what's on your computer as confidential and only look at what the repair requires. Before any work that could affect your data, I'll talk with you about backing it up first.

What areas do you cover?

Ventura County and the Conejo Valley, out to Calabasas: Thousand Oaks, Westlake Village, Oak Park, Newbury Park, Hidden Valley, Calabasas, Camarillo, Moorpark, Simi Valley, Ventura, Oxnard, Port Hueneme and nearby. Outside that area? Call or text and ask.

A pop-up says my computer is infected and to call a number. What should I do?

Don't call the number in the pop-up, don't let anyone you don't know connect to your computer, and never pay with gift cards or a wire transfer. Close the browser or restart the computer. If you gave them a card number or bank login, call your bank right away. If the pop-up keeps coming back, or you already let someone in, call or text (805) 622-7303 and book a malware check. Seniors get 25% off home jobs.
