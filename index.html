Questions

Good to know

Do you take walk-ins?

No. Code Blue works by appointment only, so each repair gets full attention and you aren't left waiting in line.

Will I know the cost before you start?

Yes. After a diagnosis you get a quote, and no repair work starts until you approve it.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes. Code Blue IT Services handles small-business networks, workstations, Microsoft 365, backups, and security, either per visit, on a schedule, or as a fixed-price project.

Is my data safe with you?

Your files stay private. I only access what's needed for the repair, and I'll back up important data before any work that could affect it.

What areas do you cover?

Ventura County and the Conejo Valley, out to Calabasas: Thousand Oaks, Westlake Village, Oak Park, Newbury Park, Camarillo, Moorpark, Simi Valley, Ventura, Oxnard and nearby. Outside that area? Call or text and ask.
